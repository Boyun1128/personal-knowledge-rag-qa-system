Title: Gemma 4 — Google DeepMind Open-Weight Multimodal AI
Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core/model_card_4
Source Type: Official Model Card + Google DeepMind Announcement
Category: Google Gemma Updates, LLM / Foundation Models, Multimodal Models
Date: April 2, 2026

Google DeepMind officially released Gemma 4 on April 2, 2026, marking a major milestone in open-source AI. This family of models delivers frontier-level intelligence per parameter, built from the same research and technology powering Gemini 3.

Model Sizes and Variants:
- E2B: Edge 2B parameters, suitable for smartphones
- E4B: Edge 4B parameters, balanced edge deployment
- 26B A4B: 26B total parameters, 4B active (Mixture-of-Experts)
- 31B: Dense 31B parameter model, fits on single 80GB H100 GPU

Key Architectural Innovations:
1. Mixture-of-Experts (MoE): The 26B A4B variant uses MoE, activating only 4B parameters per token
2. Full multimodal: text, image, video, and audio input support
3. Audio support on small models (E2B, E4B)
4. Hybrid attention mechanism
5. Progressive Layer Expansion (PLE) for efficient scaling

License Change:
- Apache 2.0 license (most permissive Gemma license to date)
- Previous Gemma versions used a custom Gemma license
- Apache 2.0 enables broader commercial use and modification

Performance:
- Benchmark scores rival models 20x their size
- Runs frontier AI on a single GPU (31B on 80GB H100)
- Competitive with GPT-4 class models on many benchmarks
- Designed for agentic workflows and complex reasoning

Deployment Scenarios:
- E2B/E4B: Smartphones, embedded devices, edge inference
- 26B A4B: Workstation-class deployment with MoE efficiency
- 31B: Single high-end GPU server deployment

Agentic Capabilities:
- Designed specifically for autonomous agentic workflows
- Multi-step planning and reasoning
- Tool use without fine-tuning
- Complex reasoning chains

Availability:
- Hugging Face: google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it, google/gemma-4-31B-it, etc.
- Google AI Studio
- Vertex AI
- Kaggle

Why Gemma 4 Represents AI Rapid Evolution:
- Released just 13 months after Gemma 3
- Jumped from decoder-only to full multimodal (text+image+video+audio)
- Introduced MoE architecture for the first time in Gemma family
- License became fully open-source (Apache 2.0)
- Frontier-level performance now accessible on consumer hardware
- Demonstrates the compression of capabilities: what required massive compute in 2023 now runs on a phone
