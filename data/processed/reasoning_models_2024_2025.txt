Title: Reasoning Models: Chain-of-Thought and Extended Thinking (2024-2025)
Source: Multiple technical sources including arXiv papers and official announcements
Source Type: Technical Overview
Category: LLM / Foundation Models, Trend Signals / Important Releases
Date: 2024-2025

Reasoning models represent a major advancement in LLM capabilities, enabling complex multi-step problem solving through extended thinking before answering.

What are Reasoning Models:
- LLMs trained to "think before answering"
- Generate intermediate reasoning steps (chain-of-thought)
- Significantly better on math, coding, and logical reasoning
- Trade-off: slower and more expensive than standard models

Key Reasoning Models:

OpenAI o1 (September 2024):
- First major commercial reasoning model
- "Thinks" before answering using internal chain-of-thought
- Significantly better than GPT-4 on math and coding
- AIME 2024: 83.3% (vs. GPT-4o: 13.4%)
- Slower and more expensive than GPT-4o

OpenAI o3 (December 2024):
- Improved reasoning over o1
- Near-human performance on ARC-AGI benchmark
- Expensive: $15-60 per million tokens

DeepSeek R1 (January 2025):
- Open-source reasoning model
- Comparable to o1 on many benchmarks
- Training cost: ~$5.6M (vs. estimated hundreds of millions for o1)
- Full weights released publicly
- Distilled versions: 1.5B to 70B parameters

Claude 3.5 Sonnet with Extended Thinking (2025):
- Anthropic's reasoning-capable model
- Extended thinking: visible reasoning process
- Strong on coding and analysis tasks

Gemini 2.5 Pro (2025):
- Google's reasoning model
- Strong on math and science benchmarks
- Integrated with Google products

How Reasoning Works:

Chain-of-Thought (CoT):
- Model generates step-by-step reasoning
- "Let me think about this step by step..."
- Improves accuracy on complex tasks
- Can be prompted or trained

Extended Thinking / Scratchpad:
- Model has a private "thinking" space
- Reasoning not shown to user (or shown separately)
- More compute = better reasoning
- Inference-time scaling: more thinking = better answers

Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning:
- DeepSeek R1 approach: RL from scratch
- Model learns to reason through trial and error
- No supervised fine-tuning on reasoning traces
- Emergent reasoning capabilities

Distillation:
- Train small models to mimic large reasoning models
- DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B: 8B model with reasoning
- Enables reasoning on consumer hardware

Benchmarks for Reasoning:

AIME (American Invitational Mathematics Examination):
- High school math competition problems
- Very challenging for LLMs
- DeepSeek R1: 79.8%, OpenAI o1: 83.3%

MATH-500:
- 500 competition math problems
- DeepSeek R1: 97.3%

ARC-AGI:
- Abstract reasoning benchmark
- Tests general intelligence
- OpenAI o3: near-human performance

Codeforces:
- Competitive programming problems
- Tests algorithmic reasoning

Implications for AI Rapid Evolution:
- Reasoning models emerged as a major paradigm in 2024
- Open-source caught up to commercial in <6 months (DeepSeek R1)
- Inference-time scaling: a new axis of improvement beyond training
- The "best" approach to reasoning is still evolving
- Researchers must track both training-time and inference-time advances
