Title: DeepSeek R1 and the Cost Revolution in AI (January 2025)
Source: https://solvedtogether.co.uk/blog/latest-ai-advancements (and related coverage)
Source Type: Technical Analysis Article
Category: LLM / Foundation Models, Trend Signals / Important Releases
Date: January 2025

DeepSeek's R1 model, released in January 2025, disrupted the AI industry by achieving performance comparable to leading Western models at dramatically lower training and inference costs.

What is DeepSeek R1:
- Open-source reasoning model from Chinese AI lab DeepSeek
- Achieves performance comparable to OpenAI o1 on reasoning benchmarks
- Training cost estimated at ~$5.6 million (vs. hundreds of millions for comparable Western models)
- Released with full weights and technical report
- Available on Hugging Face

Key Technical Innovations:
1. Reinforcement Learning from scratch: trained reasoning capabilities via RL without supervised fine-tuning
2. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO): efficient RL algorithm
3. Chain-of-thought reasoning: extended thinking before answering
4. Mixture-of-Experts architecture: efficient parameter utilization
5. Distillation: smaller DeepSeek-R1 distilled models (1.5B to 70B)

Performance Benchmarks:
- AIME 2024 (math): 79.8% (comparable to OpenAI o1)
- MATH-500: 97.3%
- Codeforces: 96.3rd percentile
- MMLU: 90.8%
- Competitive with GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on many tasks

Why DeepSeek R1 Shocked the Industry:
1. Cost efficiency: frontier performance at 1/10th the assumed cost
2. Open weights: full model weights released publicly
3. Reproducibility: detailed technical report enabled replication
4. Challenged assumptions: showed that massive compute is not required for frontier AI
5. Geopolitical implications: demonstrated non-US labs can reach frontier

Impact on AI Ecosystem:
- Triggered re-evaluation of AI development cost assumptions
- Accelerated open-source reasoning model development
- Inspired distilled versions (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen)
- Showed that RL-based reasoning training is accessible to smaller labs
- Caused significant stock market reactions in AI-adjacent companies

The Rapid Iteration Context:
- DeepSeek V2 (May 2024) → DeepSeek V3 (December 2024) → DeepSeek R1 (January 2025)
- Three major releases in 8 months
- Each release significantly advanced the state of the art
- Demonstrates the global, distributed nature of AI progress
- No single lab or country controls the pace of advancement

Implications for Knowledge Management:
- A model that didn't exist in December 2024 became a top-tier option by February 2025
- Researchers who weren't tracking Chinese AI labs missed a major development
- Highlights the need for comprehensive, multi-source knowledge tracking
- Traditional academic channels (peer review) too slow to capture this pace
