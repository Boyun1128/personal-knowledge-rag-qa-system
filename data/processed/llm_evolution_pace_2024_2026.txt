Title: LLM Model Evolution 2024-2026: The Accelerating Pace of AI Development
Source: https://www.llm-evolution.com/ and https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/state-of-llms-2025
Source Type: Technical Analysis
Category: Trend Signals / Important Releases, LLM / Foundation Models
Date: 2025-2026

The pace of LLM development has accelerated dramatically, with the "imitation cycle" — the time for competitors to match a new model's capabilities — compressing from 12 months to 6 weeks.

Timeline of Major LLM Milestones (2022-2026):

2022:
- November: ChatGPT released (GPT-3.5)
- Inference cost: $20 per million tokens for GPT-3.5 equivalent

2023:
- March: GPT-4 released
- July: Llama 2 (Meta) — open-source milestone
- September: Mistral 7B — efficient open-source model
- October: GPT-4V — vision capabilities

2024:
- February: Gemma 1 (Google) — first open Gemini-based model
- April: Llama 3 (Meta) — 8B, 70B, 405B
- June: Gemma 2 (Google)
- June: Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- July: Llama 3.1 405B — competitive with GPT-4
- September: Qwen 2.5 (Alibaba)
- October: Inference cost: $0.07 per million tokens (280x reduction in 18 months)
- December: DeepSeek V3

2025:
- January: DeepSeek R1 — frontier reasoning at 1/10th cost
- March: Gemma 3 — multimodal, 128K context
- March: GPT-4.5
- April: Llama 4 (Meta)
- May: Claude 4
- June: Gemini 2.5 Pro
- December: FunctionGemma, T5Gemma 2

2026:
- April: Gemma 4 — Apache 2.0, MoE, full multimodal

Key Observations:

Capability Compression:
- What required GPT-4 in 2023 can be done by a 7B open-source model in 2025
- Multimodal capabilities: commercial-only in 2023, open-source in 2025
- 128K context: rare in 2023, standard in 2025
- Reasoning: specialized in 2024, widespread in 2025

Cost Collapse:
- 280x cost reduction in 18 months (2022-2024)
- Trend continues in 2025-2026
- Enables applications that were economically infeasible 2 years ago

The Knowledge Half-Life Problem:
- A researcher who learned about "state-of-the-art" models in early 2024 would be significantly outdated by late 2024
- The "best" open-source model changes approximately every 2-3 months
- Technical skills for specific models become obsolete quickly
- Framework APIs change frequently (LangChain, LlamaIndex breaking changes)

Implications for Researchers and Practitioners:
1. Continuous learning is mandatory, not optional
2. Generalist knowledge (architectures, training methods) more durable than specific model knowledge
3. Community resources (Hugging Face, Papers With Code, arXiv) essential for staying current
4. RAG systems for knowledge management are increasingly valuable
5. The ability to quickly evaluate new models is a competitive advantage

The Scaling Law Debate (2024-2025):
- 2024: researchers began questioning whether scaling laws would continue
- DeepSeek R1 showed efficiency gains can substitute for scale
- Mixture-of-Experts: more parameters, same compute
- Inference-time compute (chain-of-thought): alternative to training-time scale
- Conclusion: scaling continues, but the form is changing

What This Means for AI Knowledge Management:
- Static knowledge bases become outdated within months
- Need for systems that can be updated incrementally (like data_update.py)
- Citation and source tracking essential for verifying currency of information
- The RAG system itself is a response to the knowledge management challenge
